IP Atlas Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

IP Atlas ("the app") is published by Code Compiler. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. In short: no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no GPS, and your lookup history never leaves your phone.

1. Information the app sends

To show IP details, the app sends a request to ip-api.com, a third-party IP geolocation service. Each request contains:

  • Your IP address, which every internet request carries automatically. It is used to show your own public IP and its approximate (city-level) location.
  • The IP address or domain you type into the search bar, so the service can return its details.

ip-api.com returns information such as country, region, city, internet provider, timezone and approximate coordinates. The app uses it only to display results to you. ip-api.com's handling of requests is described in its own policy: ip-api.com/docs/legal.

The free ip-api.com service is reached over a standard (HTTP) connection, which is not encrypted.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Logbook: your past lookups are saved only on your phone so you can view them again. You can delete single entries, clear everything in Settings, or switch saving off.
  • Settings: your theme choice and Logbook preference are saved on your phone.

Uninstalling the app removes all of this data.

3. What the app does not do

  • It does not ask for or use location permission or GPS.
  • It does not require an account and does not collect your name, email, contacts, photos or files.
  • It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • It does not sell or rent any information.
  • We (the developer) do not run any server that receives your data.

4. Sharing

Information is only sent to ip-api.com, as described above, to provide the lookup feature. When you use the app's Share button, the text goes to the app you pick, under your control.

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

6. Accuracy

IP location shows where a network is registered, usually the city or region of an internet provider. It is not a street address and must not be used to identify or locate a person.

7. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the effective date above will change.
